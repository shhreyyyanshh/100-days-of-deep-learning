# Day 1
  
## Topics Covered
- Artificial Intelligence
- Machine Learning
- Deep Learning
- AI vs ML vs DL

## What I Learned
- AI is the broader field of creating systems capable of performing tasks that usually require human intelligence.
- Machine Learning is a subset of AI where models learn patterns from data.
- Deep Learning is a subset of Machine Learning that uses neural networks with multiple layers.

## My Biggest Takeaway
Deep Learning isn't separate from Machine Learning—it's a specialized approach within it.
